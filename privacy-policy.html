We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information BUNORA may collect through this website and how we use it.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Information you share with us

If you contact us, subscribe to our newsletter, or make an inquiry, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of your message. Please do not send payment card details or sensitive personal information through general contact forms.

Information collected by the website

Our hosting and security services may process basic technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. This website also stores your theme and language-direction preferences in your browser's local storage so they can be remembered on your next visit.

How we use information

  • To respond to questions and fulfill the requests you send us.
  • To send updates you have requested, with an option to unsubscribe where applicable.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the website and our communications.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of our guests and business.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with service providers that help us operate the website or communications, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users. We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you have provided, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Your rights depend on where you live. Contact us at hello@bunorarestaurant.com and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Children and policy updates

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version and its effective date will appear on this page.

Contact
