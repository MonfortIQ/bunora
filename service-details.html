Party & Gathering Catering

Elevate birthdays, celebrations, watch parties, and social reunions with BUNORA's crowd-pleasing gourmet slider stations, artisan deli platters, and loaded sides.

Whether you are hosting an intimate 15-person dinner or a vibrant 250-person festival, our culinary catering team delivers food that looks as spectacular as it tastes. We provide commercial stainless chafing dishes to keep sliders and meats piping hot, compostable bamboo dining wares, and customizable condiment stations.

Why Choose BUNORA Catering?

Hot Platter Delivery

Insulated thermal transport ensures burgers and sides arrive fresh and crispy.

Custom Slider Variety

Mix and match Wagyu beef, fried chicken, pastrami, and plant-based vegan sliders.

Full Tableware Setup

Compostable bamboo plates, birch cutlery, napkins, and serving tongs included.

Event Concierge

Dedicated coordinator assists with portion planning and schedule timing.
