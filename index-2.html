The Artisan Philosophy

Rooted in European Deli Traditions

BUNORA began not as a chain, but as a neighborhood corner bakery where the morning sun hit racks of cooling sourdough and copper pots of simmering pastrami brine.

We mill a portion of our flour in-house to capture the nutty vitality of fresh grain. When you order a sandwich, our chefs slice the pastrami and prosciutto right off the slab in front of you. No pre-cut deli meat, no artificial dough conditioners — just honest culinary passion.

48 Hours

Slow cold fermentation for optimal digestibility and complex flavor.

100% Sliced Fresh

Every deli cut shaved right upon order for maximum juiciness.
